\# Risk Dashboard



\## Purpose



This dashboard provides management-level visibility into information-security risks before and after implementation of security controls.



\## Risk Calculation



Risk Score = Likelihood × Impact



| Score | Rating |

|---:|---|

| 1–5 | Low |

| 6–10 | Medium |

| 11–15 | High |

| 16–25 | Critical |

\## Management Actions



1\. Review Critical and High inherent risks.

2\. Monitor residual Medium risks.

3\. Verify control effectiveness.

4\. Retest vulnerabilities before closure.

5\. Perform quarterly risk reviews.

6\. Document risk acceptance where applicable.

7\. Escalate risks exceeding risk tolerance.

\## Review Frequency



Risk registers should be reviewed:



\- Quarterly

\- After significant security incidents

\- After major system changes

\- After significant organizational changes

\- After major regulatory changes
