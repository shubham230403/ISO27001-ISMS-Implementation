# Internal Audit Program

## 1. Purpose

This internal audit simulation evaluates the effectiveness of the simulated Information Security Management System (ISMS) implemented for FinSecure Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

The audit determines whether selected ISMS processes and Annex A controls are defined, implemented, supported by objective evidence, operating effectively, monitored and reviewed, and subject to corrective action where gaps are identified.

> **Portfolio Disclaimer:** This is a simulated internal audit performed for a cybersecurity/GRC portfolio project. It is not an actual ISO/IEC 27001 certification audit.

## 2. Audit Criteria

The audit criteria include:

1. ISO/IEC 27001:2022 ISMS requirements
2. Applicable Annex A controls
3. FinSecure information security policies
4. Risk assessment and risk treatment process
5. Statement of Applicability
6. Control implementation records
7. Evidence repository
8. Internal control-testing results
9. CAPA records
10. Management review records

## 3. Audit Scope

The audit covers selected information-security processes supporting:

- Customer information
- Transaction information
- Production applications
- APIs
- Cloud infrastructure
- Identity and authentication
- Security monitoring
- Vulnerability management
- Backup and recovery
- Security awareness
- Incident management
- Supplier/security governance

## 4. Audit Objectives

The objectives are to:

- Verify that selected controls are implemented as documented.
- Verify that objective evidence exists.
- Determine whether selected controls operate effectively.
- Confirm that identified risks are addressed through appropriate controls.
- Verify that previous audit findings have been corrected.
- Verify CAPA effectiveness.
- Identify new improvement opportunities.
- Provide an overall internal audit conclusion.

## 5. Audit Approach

The audit uses:

### Document Review

Review policies, risk registers, control registers, evidence records, audit findings, CAPA records and management review documentation.

### Interviews

Interview responsible control owners and process owners.

### Evidence Sampling

Select representative records and verify that evidence supports the control requirement.

### Control Testing

Compare expected control requirements against available objective evidence.

### CAPA Verification

Verify that previous findings have been corrected and corrective actions are effective.

## 7. Audit Sampling Strategy

The audit uses risk-based sampling.

Higher priority is assigned to:

- Critical inherent risks
- High inherent risks
- Customer and transaction data
- Authentication systems
- Vulnerability management
- Security monitoring
- Previously identified audit findings

Evidence is sampled based on relevance, risk and control importance.

## 8. Audit Evidence Requirements

## 10. Audit Conclusion Criteria

### Conformity

The requirement is implemented and supported by sufficient objective evidence.

### Observation

The requirement is generally addressed, but an improvement opportunity exists.

### Minor Nonconformity

A limited control deficiency exists that does not indicate a complete breakdown of the ISMS process.

### Major Nonconformity

A significant systemic failure exists or a required ISMS process is not effectively implemented.

## 13. Auditor Independence

For this simulation, the auditor is treated as an independent internal reviewer who evaluates evidence against predefined audit criteria.

This simulation demonstrates internal audit methodology and does not represent an actual certification audit.

## 14. Expected Outcome

The audit should determine:

- Whether selected controls are operating effectively.
- Whether previous findings remain closed.
- Whether corrective actions are effective.
- Whether additional improvement opportunities exist.
- Whether management attention is required for remaining partially implemented controls.
