# Internal Audit Plan

## Objective

Evaluate whether the simulated ISMS processes are appropriately designed and implemented.

## Scope

- ISMS context
- ISMS scope
- Risk management
- Access control
- Vulnerability management
- Logging
- Incident management
- Supplier security
- CAPA
- Management review

## Audit Process

1. Review documents.
2. Inspect evidence.
3. Test controls.
4. Identify findings.
5. Assign corrective actions.
6. Track closure.
7. Verify effectiveness.
