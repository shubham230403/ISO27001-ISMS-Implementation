\# 3. CAPA-001 — Access Review Evidence



\*\*Finding:\*\* F-001  

\*\*Severity:\*\* Medium



\### Root Cause



Access review evidence was not consistently retained in a centralized location.



\### Corrective Action



A centralized quarterly access review process was established.



\### Evidence



`EVD-001`

The auditor reviewed the access review evidence and verified that:



\- Accounts were reviewed.

\- Exceptions were documented.

\# 4. CAPA-002 — Vulnerability Retesting



\*\*Finding:\*\* F-002  

\*\*Severity:\*\* High



\### Root Cause



The vulnerability remediation process did not require mandatory retesting evidence before closure.



\### Corrective Action



The vulnerability management workflow was updated to require:



\- Remediation

\- Retesting

\- Evidence retention

\- Closure approval



\### Evidence

\### Effectiveness Test

1\. Identified

3\. Retested

4\. Confirmed not exploitable

5\. Approved for closure

\*\*Effective\*\*

\*\*Closed\*\*

\# 5. CAPA-003 — CAPA Tracking



\*\*Finding:\*\* F-003  

\*\*Severity:\*\* High



\### Root Cause



CAPA records did not consistently document effectiveness verification.



\### Corrective Action



The CAPA process was updated to require:



\- CAPA owner

\- Target date

\- Corrective action

\- Completion evidence

\- Effectiveness verification

\- Closure approval



\### Effectiveness Test



The CAPA register was reviewed against the updated process.

\### Result



\*\*Effective\*\*



\### Closure Decision



\*\*Closed\*\*
